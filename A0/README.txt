To compile singly linked list: g++ main.cpp ll.cpp
To run: ./a.out

To compile: g++ main.cpp ll.cpp -o sll
To run: ./sll


To compile doubly linked list: g++ main_dll.cpp dll.cpp -o dll
To run: ./dll
